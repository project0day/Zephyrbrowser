PRIVACY POLICY — ZEPHYR BROWSER

Last updated: 20 September 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

Zephyr Browser has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry.
Nothing you do in this app is reported to its developer. Your browsing history,
bookmarks, passwords, downloads, notes, documents and assistant conversations
are stored on your device and nowhere else.

There is no server belonging to this app. There is nothing for us to collect,
because nothing reaches us.


1. WHAT THE APP STORES ON YOUR DEVICE

Browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs, saved passwords and payment details,
downloads, site permissions, offline pages, user scripts, assistant chats,
generated documents, templates, reminders and settings.

All of it is written to the app's own storage on your phone. It is removed when
you delete it in the app, when you use "Clear data" or the burn button, or when
you uninstall the app.

Saved passwords and payment details can be protected by your device's screen
lock or fingerprint. They are not uploaded anywhere.


2. WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE, AND WHEN

The app itself does not transmit anything about you. Some features, by their
nature, contact other parties. Each one is listed here.

2.1 Websites you open
Normal browsing. The site you open receives whatever any browser sends it.

2.2 Your search engine
When you search, the query goes to the engine you selected. If search
suggestions are enabled, what you type in the address bar is sent to that
engine as you type. You choose the engine, including private options.

2.3 Site icons
To show a site's icon, the app may request it from a third-party icon service,
which tells that service the domain name. This never happens in private tabs,
and it can be switched off entirely in Settings → Privacy.

2.4 The AI assistant
This is the important one.

The assistant does not use any service belonging to this app's developer. It
connects to whichever AI provider YOU configure, using an API key that YOU
obtain and enter. When you send a message, the text you wrote — and any page
content, file, photograph or document you attach — is sent to that provider.

That provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to what you send it. We are
not a party to it and cannot see any of it. Choose a provider you trust, and
read their policy.

The assistant is optional. Without a key it does nothing, and every individual
ability it has (reading pages, running scripts, creating files, using the
camera, using the microphone) has its own switch in Settings.

2.5 Speech recognition
If your phone has a system speech recogniser, the app uses it, and your voice
is handled by whoever provides it — on most phones, Google. You can instead
download an offline language model, after which speech is recognised entirely
on your device and no audio leaves it. As a third option you may send audio to
your own AI provider for transcription. Settings → AI assistant.

2.6 Translation
If you translate a page, its text is sent to the translation service.

2.7 Ad-blocking filter lists
The app downloads public filter lists from their publishers. It sends no
information about you — only a request for the list.

2.8 Crash reports
There is no automatic crash reporting. If a crash occurs the details are saved
to a file on your device. You may choose to send that file yourself, through an
app of your choosing. Nothing is sent unless you do this deliberately.

2.9 VPN, proxy and DNS
If you configure these, traffic goes to the servers you entered. The app
provides no servers of its own and operates none.


3. PERMISSIONS

The app asks for permissions only for features you use.

Camera — scanning QR codes, and photographing documents for the assistant.
Microphone — voice input.
Storage and media — saving downloads, opening files you select.
Notifications — download progress, reminders, and assistant replies.
Exact alarms — reminders that must fire at a specific time.
Location — only if a website asks and you allow it.

Denying any of these disables that feature and nothing else.


4. CHILDREN

This app is not directed at children.


5. CHANGES

If this policy changes, the updated text ships with the app version it applies
to, and the date above changes.
